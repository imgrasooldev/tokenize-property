// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this crypto?

No. Tokens are regulated security tokens recording ownership. Money only moves in PKR through banks and Raast.

Do I really own the building?

You own units in the trust that owns it. The trustee's register and the tokens always match.

Can the AI tell me what to buy?

No. It explains documents and data with sources. Decisions stay yours.

What if the platform shuts down?

The trustee holds the building and the register; the blockchain keeps the record. Your ownership does not depend on us.
