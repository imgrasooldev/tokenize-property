// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I actually own the property?+

You own tokens that represent units in the trust that owns the building. The trustee's register and the tokens always match, one to one.

How do I get my rent?+

Every month, your share of the rent is paid to your own bank account via Raast. You can verify the amount yourself on the blockchain.

Can I sell whenever I want?+

You can place a sell order on the market at any time; it sells when a buyer matches your price. Secondary trading needs SECP approval before launch.

What happens if the platform shuts down?+

The trustee still holds the building and the register of owners, so your ownership does not depend on us.
