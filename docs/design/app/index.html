Built in, on every screen

No keys on the phoneThe app talks only to the public API. Units sit in a custody wallet; the signing service signs with maker-checker.
Passkey for money movesInvest, withdraw, send, exit, vote and nominee changes each ask for a fresh fingerprint or face check.
Own-name money onlyPayments in and payouts out use the investor's own verified bank account; a new account waits 72 hours.
Proof you can checkDocuments, holdings and payouts carry hashes and on-chain anchors that the app re-checks.
Information, not adviceThe analyst cites documents and never recommends; the demo market is labelled until regulators approve.
