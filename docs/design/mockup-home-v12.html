// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this cryptocurrency?+

No. The blockchain only records ownership. Your money moves in PKR, through banks and Raast.

What does the AI do?+

It explains leases, valuations, risks and your rent, with sources for every claim. It never tells you what to buy.

Do I own the building?+

You own units in the trust that owns it; the trustee's register and the tokens always match.

Can I sell?+

Yes, with a sell order on the market. Secondary trading needs SECP approval before launch.
