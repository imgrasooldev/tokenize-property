FAQ

Good questions.

Do I really own the building?

You own units in the trust that owns it. The trustee's register and your tokens always match.

How is rent paid?

Monthly, to your own bank account via Raast, in Pakistani rupees.

Can I sell any time?

Yes, by placing a sell order; it completes when a buyer matches your price. Trading needs SECP approval before launch.

Is this halal?

Income comes from rent, not interest. A Shariah review of the structure is planned before launch.
